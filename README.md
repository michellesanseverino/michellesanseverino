## 👩‍💻 About Me

I transform raw data into clear business insights and efficient automated workflows — from data pipelines to automated web scrapers using **Python**, **Pandas**, **SQL**, **Power BI**, and **AWS**.

I hold a degree in **Journalism**, an **MBA in Agile Management**, and a postgraduate degree in **UX Design**. With 4+ years of experience as a **Technical Writer in Tech**, I bring a unique edge to data analysis: the ability to build clean data models, write clear documentation, and communicate complex data findings through effective visual storytelling.

- 📬 Target roles: **Data Analyst · Python Developer · Analytics Engineer**
- 🧠 My superpower: Technical expertise (Python & Data) + Clear Business Communication & Storytelling
